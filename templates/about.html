What we do

{{ site.name }} brings vehicle buyers together. When enough people want the same car, bike or scooter, they form a group, and dealers compete to give that group their best price. A bigger group means a stronger offer.

How it works

  • Pick a vehicle. Browse cars, bikes, scooters and electric scooters, and show your interest.
  • Join a group. Reserve your seat in a group of buyers in your city.
  • Compare dealer offers. Dealers send offers for the whole group. You see the price and delivery time and vote for the offer you like.
  • We follow the deal. Once an offer is confirmed, we track it through contact, booking and delivery.

More than just the deal

Use our EMI calculator and loan help, get insurance quotes, sell your used vehicle, or book a one-to-one consultation with our team before you decide.

What we stand for

  • Fair prices: Dealers compete for your group, so you do not have to bargain alone.
  • Your privacy: Dealer names and your contact details are not shown to the public.
  • Honest reviews: Reviews are shown publicly only when the customer allows it and our team approves them.
